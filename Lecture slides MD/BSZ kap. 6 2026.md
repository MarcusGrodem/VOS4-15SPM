### Slide 14: The Cournot Model

- Duopolists A and B face industry demand P = 100-Q, Q = QA+QB Each firm takes the other’s output as fixed, and then decides how much to produce! E.g., PA = (100-QB*)-QA Marginal revenue for A is MRA = (100-QB*)-2QA If MC = 0, the optimal output for A for the given output for B is (1) QA = 50 - 0,5QB (2) QB = 50 - 0,5QA ..which is the reaction curve for firm A (henholdsvis B)
